<objective>
Put the stream on the wire: a per-request middleware that clears the absolute write deadline
for exactly one path, a handler that registers a subscriber and sends until the client goes
away, and the end-to-end proof that the whole path delivers message by message.

Purpose: two traps decide whether this works at all. The server's `writeTimeout` is an
ABSOLUTE deadline on a whole response, so an untouched stream dies at 60 seconds; and
connect-go's `checkServerStreamsCanFlush` REJECTS a streaming handler whose response writer
does not implement the flusher interface, so a middleware that wraps the writer in a custom
type does not merely lose flushing — it stops the handler being served at all.

Output: the transport half of live push, and the phase's tracer assertion.
</objective>
