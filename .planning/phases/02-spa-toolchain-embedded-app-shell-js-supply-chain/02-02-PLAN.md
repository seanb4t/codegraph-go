    Order of operations in `ServeHTTP`:
    1. Reject any method other than `GET` and `HEAD` with 405 and an `Allow` header.
    2. Set `X-Content-Type-Options` to `nosniff` AND `Content-Security-Policy` to `spaCSPPolicy`
