| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|

| a recorded decision → future readers of the perf gate | `tools/bench/BASELINE.md` is the authority a future rebless is judged against; a decision recorded wrongly misleads every later measurement |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-01-05-01 | Elevation of Privilege | `workflow_dispatch` against a historical ref | medium | mitigate | The dispatch runs the workflow definition as it existed at the historical commit, which predates later hardening. Gated behind a blocking human-action checkpoint, scoped to one run, against a ref pushed from a sha already in this repository's history (no fork, no external content), and the ref is deleted immediately afterward. |
