<objective>
Remove the `getppid` test seam's package-level mutable global and the watchdog's real
wall-clock dependency, so FIX-08's race is structurally impossible and FIX-07's flake has
no timer left to lose against.

Purpose: the v0.3.0 Phase 4 fix (`testBudget` + `joinDaemonRun`) reduced the race's
likelihood through join discipline but left the global in place and left the plain-timeout
tail under load — its own SUMMARY says so. `GOTOOLCHAIN=go1.26.6 go test -race -count=1 ./...`
fails `TestRunWatchdogCancelsRunOnSimulatedReparent` on this tree today (250.35 s, zero data
races, reproduced 2026-09-14). This plan finishes the job at the design defect.

Output: a per-instance seam, an injectable tick source, an AST-level shape guard that makes
a future reintroduction of the global fail loudly, and a recorded verdict on GH #13's
second half.

**TDD note:** this repo's Go tests are not TAP, so GSD's `tdd-red-evidence` TAP parser cannot
read them. RED→GREEN is carried by this plan's own Go-native verify gates plus pasted
transcripts, per the repo's recorded caveat.
</objective>
