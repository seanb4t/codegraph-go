<objective>
Render the coverage denominator on `/health`: the counts line, reason groups that expand to per-file rows, extraction failures visibly distinct from exclusions, and a first-class "Coverage unknown — re-index to record it" state for pre-Phase-10 graphs — all text, no action controls — with vitest coverage for every claim and the SPA rebuilt drift-clean.

Purpose: HLT-04 is judged by what the user sees; the projection must present exactly the numbers the server recorded (never re-derived), name every gap with its recorded reason, and make the unknown state impossible to mistake for zero.

</objective>
