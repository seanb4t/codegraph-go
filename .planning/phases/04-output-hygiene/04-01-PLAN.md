<objective>
Route Pebble's internal WAL/INFO log noise away from every command's output while
preserving real error and fatal diagnostics (HYG-01). Introduce a small unexported
`quietLogger` implementing pebble v2's three-method `base.Logger` interface, injected
at the module's single `pebble.Open` seam.

Purpose: Close the v1.0 parity gap where `&pebble.Options{}` lets Pebble's
`DefaultLogger` print WAL/compaction/job-queue chatter on stderr for every command
that opens the store. Do NOT wholesale-silence — route INFO→discard explicitly so
store-corruption errors are never hidden (ROADMAP guardrail, D-02).

Output: `internal/graphstore/logger.go` (quietLogger + diagWriter seam),
`internal/graphstore/logger_test.go` (behavioral + mutation-proof wiring tests), and
a one-field change at `internal/graphstore/pebble_store.go:147`.
</objective>
