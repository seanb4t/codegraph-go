<objective>
Define the schema-versioned, forward-compatible record format that makes ARCH-01 true. Author `graph.proto` with proto3 `Node`, `Edge`, `File`, and `Meta` messages — each reserving field-number ranges for the post-v1 annotations named in D-03 (embedding vectors, community/cluster assignments) — generate the Go types, stamp `schema_version` in `Meta`, and prove with a round-trip test that a record carrying unknown/future fields survives an older reader losslessly.

Purpose: Protobuf's field-numbered wire format + `reserved` keyword + unknown-field preservation is the literal mechanism behind ARCH-01's "round-trips future node/edge annotation fields without a format break" (D-02). This plan owns the serialization layer only; the bulk-export half of ARCH-01 lands in Plan 01-06.
Output: `graph.proto`, generated `graph.pb.go`, `meta.go`, and `roundtrip_test.go`.
</objective>
