<objective>
Collapse today's three-job, two-runner-class release pipeline — `build` (4-leg matrix) →
`assemble` (linux amd64) → `provenance` — into one job on one runner class that invokes
`goreleaser release` exactly once, and delete every hand-rolled shell step whose work GoReleaser's
own pipes now do.

Purpose: REL-06 and REL-07. The hand-rolled `sha256sum` step and `.goreleaser.yaml`'s `checksum:`
block must never both be live — that is the second-writer bug REL-07 exists to make structurally
impossible. Their deletion and activation are one atomic contract, not two independent changes.
Collapsing the jobs also removes the natural boundary that made "which job holds the OIDC token"
easy to eyeball, so this plan replaces the eyeball with a machine check (D-11).

The `provenance:` job is deliberately left in place here and replaced in plan 01-04, which owns
the attestor swap and its one-way decision gate.

Output: one `release` job, a `release:goreleaser` Taskfile target, and three shape tests — one
rewritten, two new — each demonstrated RED.
</objective>
