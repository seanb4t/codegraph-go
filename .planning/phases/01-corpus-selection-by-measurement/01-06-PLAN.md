<objective>
Run the spike. Fetch and index the candidates, record what each actually produces, choose and
freeze the per-kind threshold from the measured distributions, and lock the corpus set on that
evidence.

Purpose: this is the phase's reason for existing. The trap it exists to catch is already in
this repository's history — v1.0 Phase 1 established that codegraph-go's own idiomatic Go
source legitimately produces **zero** `overrides` and **zero** `type_of` edges, so a corpus set
can under-cover the nine-kind vocabulary while every test stays green. That is a silently
narrowed oracle, and it is exactly what a re-baseline invites. This plan replaces reputation
with recorded measurement.

Output: `corpora/measurements.json` (source of truth), `docs/CORPUS-MEASUREMENT.md`
(generated), and `corpora/manifest.json` with the locked flags flipped on the justified set.

Ordering is deliberate: `overrides` and `type_of` are measured FIRST, because they are the two
kinds most likely to come up short and the ones whose failure would force the widest search.
De-risking them before spending the sweep on languages that are near-certain to pass is what
keeps the bounded candidate search inside its budget.
</objective>

## Assumption delta — recorded outcome

The `assumption-delta` detector returned `detected: true` for this phase with one signal: the
term `chosen`, matched in "Fetch-at-pinned-SHA was chosen over vendoring specifically to avoid
adding red…". That match is a **false positive**: the sentence is ROADMAP prose describing a
scoping decision already made before this phase began, not a derived-to-chosen transition in
the phase's own model. It is dismissed here rather than only in reasoning, so the dismissal is
visible in the artifact.

**However, a genuine transition does exist in this phase, and it is this plan's subject.**

- **Noun that is now primary:** the **per-kind coverage threshold**. Until this plan it is a
  *derived* property of the data — the roadmap states the bar as "non-zero", which is a
  predicate computed from whatever the corpus happens to contain. D-14 and D-15 turn it into a
  *chosen*, frozen parameter: a per-kind numeric threshold selected against the measured
  distributions and written into the record with its rationale. The same transition applies,
  secondarily, to the **corpus pins**: the only existing precedent in the tree
  (`pinnedWeftCommit` in `testdata/golden/golden_parity_test.go`) is a hardcoded Go constant
  recording whatever HEAD happened to be at capture time; this phase makes the pins
  manifest-supplied, reviewed, chosen values with a single authority (D-09).

- **Decision:** `promote`.

- **Rationale:** the chosen threshold becomes the primary bar and the derived "non-zero" floor
  is subsumed rather than carried alongside — a suite that clears a per-kind threshold also
  clears non-zero, so no roadmap criterion is lost by promoting. Carrying both would mean two
  bars to keep in agreement and a guard that could pass one while failing the other. The
  promotion is recorded in `corpora/measurements.json` (threshold plus rationale) and enforced
  by a single at-least comparison in Plan 01-07's guard.

- **Accepted debt:** none from this decision. The one asymmetry deliberately left standing is
  that the five priority-4 languages keep the derived non-zero bar rather than a chosen
  threshold. That is a recorded deferral from the phase discussion, not an oversight, and it is
  stated in the record. What would force a later promotion there: a pin bump that leaves a
  priority language technically non-zero but too thin to exercise its extractor — at which
  point the language bar earns the same treatment the edge kinds get here.
</assumption_delta_decision>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Third-party repositories at pinned SHAs → this repository's indexer and its committed record | Untrusted source is fetched, parsed, and its measured properties become a committed, published artifact |
| Live GitHub licence and commit APIs → the locked set | Network-sourced facts become the basis of a legal and reproducibility claim |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-01-06-01 | Repudiation | the coverage claim | high | mitigate | A claim asserted from expectation rather than measurement is the exact failure this phase exists to prevent. Mitigated by requiring every coverage entry to name a supplying repository AND its measured count, by requiring the threshold rationale to be non-empty, and by an acceptance criterion that verifies each of the nine entries individually rather than checking that the map is merely present. |
| T-01-06-02 | Tampering | silent synthetic substitution | high | mitigate | Synthetic coverage is weaker evidence and must not read as equivalent (D-16). The record carries an explicit per-kind synthetic flag, the generated document labels it, and the prose renderer has a dedicated test asserting the label appears. |
| T-01-06-03 | Tampering | licence drift between seeding and locking | medium | mitigate | Licences are re-verified live against the GitHub licence API at lock time, not carried forward from the seeding pass. An entry whose licence is no longer MIT or Apache-2.0 fails the acceptance criterion and cannot be locked. |
| T-01-06-04 | Information Disclosure | committed measurement artifacts | medium | mitigate | Inherited from Plan 01-05: `StripVolatile` removes both path fields and every per-machine value before anything is written, and an acceptance criterion re-checks the produced record for those keys. |
| T-01-06-05 | Denial of Service | indexing large third-party monorepos | low | accept | Whole-repository pinning (D-10) means some candidates are large. The cost is bounded by the six-candidate search bound and by `apache/arrow`'s pre-measurement disqualification, and it is paid on a path-filtered CI job rather than on every push (D-08). |
| T-01-06-SC | Tampering | third-party source at pinned SHAs | medium | mitigate | Every corpus is pinned to an exact commit, never a floating branch or tag, so a compromised upstream commit landing after the pin is inert. Corpora are read-only indexer inputs and are never executed or built. No package-manager install runs in this phase, so the npm/pip/cargo Package Legitimacy Gate does not apply — recorded rather than omitted. |
</threat_model>
