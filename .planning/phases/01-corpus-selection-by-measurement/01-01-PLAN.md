<objective>
Add the measuring instrument's data layer: `StatusResult` gains a live per-edge-kind tally
(`edgesByKind`) and stops suppressing `filesByLanguage` from `--json`. This is the phase's
tracer — it wires one path end-to-end from the Pebble store through `Engine.Status`,
`MarshalStatusJSON` and the real `codegraph status --json` command, and proves the numbers
FIXT-01 needs actually come out.

Purpose: FIXT-01 cannot be satisfied without this. Scouting established the load-bearing
fact recorded in CONTEXT.md: **no per-edge-kind counting surface exists anywhere in the
tree.** Both halves of FIXT-01's data (per-kind edge counts, per-language file counts) become
readable from one `status --json` call once this lands — `filesByLanguage` is already
*computed* today and merely discarded at the JSON boundary.

Output: `internal/query/status.go` carries `EdgesByKind`, an emitted `filesByLanguage`, an
updated per-key decision table, and a truthful `Status()` cost note. A new test file pins the
tally's correctness and its sparse (absent-not-zero) semantics.
</objective>
