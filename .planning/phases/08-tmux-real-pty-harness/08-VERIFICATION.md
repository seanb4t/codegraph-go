**Judgment 1 — Is TTY-06's unproven non-vacuity a phase gap?**
No. ROADMAP.md's Success Criterion 4 ("an idle picker holds frame-stable across N captures, with N reported (TTY-05, TTY-06)") is stated as a functional behavior only — it carries no "watched fail" clause, unlike Criterion 3, which explicitly says "both watched fail against the historical G-07-1 / G-07-2 behaviour" for TTY-03/TTY-04. REQUIREMENTS.md TTY-06's own line ("A flicker proxy asserts frame stability across N captures on an idle picker") is satisfied by the code as it stands and passes on direct re-execution. D-06's ambition to also RED-prove TTY-05/TTY-06 was explicitly self-imposed, going beyond what either ROADMAP or REQUIREMENTS.md demand for this requirement — the log itself says as much ("D-06's *additional*, self-imposed ambition to also watch TTY-05 and TTY-06 fail"). Rule 84d1gfpywd ("a guard must carry a positive assertion that it did its work") concerns the *shape* of the assertion — positive, not absence-based — which `TestInstallPickerFrameStableWhileIdle` satisfies (it logs the positive count `N=5` and fails loudly on any divergence); it does not itself mandate a mutation-log RED proof for every guard, and Phase 7's own D-07 precedent (07-CONTEXT.md) explicitly declines expanding scope to force a new assertion just because a gap was found. The log's own root-cause account was independently re-derived from the code, not merely trusted: `frame_stability_test.go` genuinely calls `pollUntilStable` exactly once before the 5-capture loop and sends no further input, so a Program with `Init()` returning no `tea.Cmd` structurally cannot re-render after settling regardless of `AltScreen`'s value — this is not a rationalization, it is a correct, verifiable structural claim. **Verdict: not a gap.** It is, however, a real and now-recorded limitation of TTY-06's discriminating power against inline-vs-altscreen defects specifically confined to the settling transient — worth a human decision on whether a follow-up phase should add a settling-transient-observing assertion (already flagged by the executors in `.planning/WINDOWS.md` per 08-04-SUMMARY.md).
