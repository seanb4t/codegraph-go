<objective>

capture wrappers, the bounded stability poll — plus the first real assertion class (the empty-registry
escape-hygiene case that closes G-07-1) and the `task test:tmux` target whose exact-count gate proves
the suite actually executed.

Purpose: this is the phase's tracer. It proves the entire mechanism — `//go:build tmux` through
`TestMain`'s local build, through a real tmux pane, through `capture-pane`, through the stability poll,
through a real assertion, out to a Taskfile target that counts executed cases — on one thin path,
before three more assertion classes are built on top of it. If the mechanism is wrong, it is wrong
after one commit rather than after ten.

Output: a working, build-tagged `test/tmux` package with two passing test functions and a
`task test:tmux` target that reports an executed count and a skipped count on every run.
