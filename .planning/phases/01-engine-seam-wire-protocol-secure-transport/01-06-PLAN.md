<objective>
Build the UI's full read-only RPC surface out from the tracer's single method.

Purpose: plan 01-01 proved one path end-to-end. Everything after it is expansion along a
shape that is already known to work. This plan brings the other nine reads onto the wire —
including the two that only became structured in plan 01-04 — and makes "read-only by
construction" an asserted property rather than a stated intention.

Eight of the Engine's ten read methods already return structured results (`Search`,
`Query`, `Callers`, `Callees`, `Impact`, `Affected`, `Files`, `Status`), so their mapping
is mechanical. The two that did not — `Node` and `Explore` — now have `NodeDetail` and
`ExploreResult` behind them, which is why this plan cannot start before 01-04 lands and is
proven golden-clean.

Output: a complete `codegraph.ui.v1.UIService`, handler implementations that all go
through one gather path, and a set-equality guard that no mutating method exists.
</objective>
