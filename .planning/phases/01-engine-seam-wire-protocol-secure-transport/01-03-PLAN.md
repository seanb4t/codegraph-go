<objective>
Fix `internal/mcp/server.go`'s `pendingWriter` counter corruption (CR-01, scoped here as
FIX-01), and separately prove that fixing it does not close the `toolslist-repeat`
ordering flake.

Purpose: `pendingWriter.Write` calls `p.pending.Add(-1)` on EVERY write, while only
client-initiated request lines increment it inside `stdinLingerReader.Read`. Server-initiated
notifications — `notifications/tools/list_changed`,
`notifications/subscriptions/acknowledged` — go through the identical writer, so one
landing between a request's acceptance and its response being written drives the counter
to zero early, shortens `waitForDrain`'s effective wait, and silently loses a
still-in-flight response. Confirmed reachable, not theoretical.

This lands in Phase 1 deliberately out of dependency order: its root cause —
server-initiated writes decrementing a counter only client-initiated requests increment —
is exactly the shape Phase 6's ConnectRPC server-streaming lifecycle can reinvent, so
fixing it first makes it a worked example that phase's design review checks against.

Output: a balanced counter, a committed reproduction, and a recorded disproof of the
folded-todo hypothesis.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| MCP client → `codegraph serve --mcp` stdin | An agent process feeds newline-delimited JSON-RPC across the process boundary. `stdinLingerReader` sniffs those bytes before go-sdk's decoder sees them. |
| server → client stdout | Both client-owed responses and server-initiated notifications share one writer. Conflating them is the defect. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-01-09 | Repudiation | `internal/mcp/server.go` `pendingWriter` | high | mitigate | Decrement only on outbound lines classified as responses by `looksLikeJSONRPCResponse`; clamp at zero with a compare-and-swap loop; classify per complete newline-delimited message rather than per `Write` call. Proven by a reproduction demonstrated RED before the fix. |
| T-01-14 | Denial of Service | `waitForDrain` under an over-counting sniff | medium | accept | The conservative default — an unparseable outbound line does not decrement — can only lengthen the wait, bounded by `stdinLingerGrace` at 5 seconds. That asymmetry is chosen deliberately: a bounded wait is strictly preferable to a lost response. |
| T-01-15 | Tampering | malformed inbound line driving the sniff | low | accept | `looksLikeJSONRPCCall` already reports true conservatively on a parse failure, and the sniff reads at most `method` and `id`. No new parsing surface is introduced. |
