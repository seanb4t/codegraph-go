<objective>
Make CI unable to silently stop running the golden suite (FIXT-03) by (a) giving the golden tests a positive executed-scenario-count self-assertion that is both derived from the authoritative tables (26 goldens + 4 CASES.json cases = 30) and keyed to the loop-executed counters (review finding #1), and (b) running the golden suite only in a new unconditional job in the corpus-aware corpora.yml workflow that fetches and asserts the pinned corpora first, never gated on cache-hit, with -count=1 defeating the go test cache.

Purpose: The goldens are byte-frozen, but a suite nobody runs, or a suite whose tests all short-circuit, reports green vacuously (rule 84d1gfpywd). This plan wires the "the suite actually ran N scenarios" claim end-to-end: the test self-asserts it (D-02, wire-oracle precedent), the Taskfile target cannot serve a cached ok (constraint 3), the workflow runs it unconditionally against the fetched corpora (D-03), the CI shape guard binds the job to single-definition task targets (constraint 2), and ci.yml stops double-running the now-corpus-dependent tests against an empty corpus root (D-04).

Output: the count self-assertion, the unconditional golden job, the widened path filter, the inScopeJobs entry, and the ci.yml reconciliation.
</objective>
