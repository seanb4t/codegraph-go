Close the guava-scale invalid-endpoints warnings at their cause and land the phase's clean-console
verdict.

Purpose: cytoscape emits one warning per edge whose rendered endpoint coordinates are non-finite,
which happens when its source and target nodes overlap so completely that the endpoint math
degenerates. Plan 01-01's guava run named the pair with coordinates and bounding boxes; plan 01-08
closed the TypeError and the stylesheet typo. This plan applies the layout change that separates the
pair, verifies it does not make either graph worse, and commits the green verdict.

coincident" hypothesis was falsified — the settled layout does not overlap, and cytoscape-elk never
routes edges at all. The warning is a first-render-tick race: cytoscape draws the pair's edges once
before their endpoints are computed. The fix is a render-flow change in `GraphCanvas.svelte` (first
paint waits for the first `layoutstop`), proven load-bearing by a RED/GREEN toggle.

Output: a render-flow change in GraphCanvas.svelte that coexists with 01-08's teardown guard, an end-of-phase human review on both corpora, and a committed green verdict.
