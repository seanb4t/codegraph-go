<objective>
Settle the two Phase-1-deferred substrate decisions the extractor cannot be built without, and freeze the record shape it writes into:

1. **Node identity (D-02/D-02a):** a pure `NodeID(kind, qualifiedName, filePath) string` producing the TS-parity `<kind>:<hex>` shape, where `<hex>` is a **collision-resistant SHA-256** digest (never MD5) truncated to 32 hex chars, built over a **length-prefixed preimage** (mirroring `keys.go`'s `appendSegment` discipline so a crafted qualified_name/path cannot bleed across segments).
2. **Schema field parity (D-03 + RESEARCH A2):** additively extend `internal/schema/graph.proto` with the Go parity fields the extractor populates now, staying below the reserved `50..59` range and keeping `SchemaVersion = 1`.

Purpose: every Node the extractor emits and every reference the resolver matches depends on a stable, reproducible id and on the presence of the parity fields. These are load-bearing for LANG-01 (field parity) and RES-01 (id recomputation is how a reference matches a symbol).

Output: `internal/indexer/nodeid` package (leaf, no in-repo deps beyond stdlib); extended `graph.proto` + regenerated `graph.pb.go`; `x/sync` and `x/mod` promoted from indirect to direct in `go.mod`.
</objective>
