## Finding 1 — Canonical verification status is `stale` for Phases 7–11 (tool artefact, not a delivery gap)

`gsd_run query verification.status <phase dir>` returns `stale` for 07, 08, 09, 10 and 11 while each file's raw `status:` is `passed`. Two causes, both mechanical:

- **Phases 7–10:** their `covered_files` include `.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md`, which every subsequent `phase.complete` rewrites (#4155). They flipped to `stale` the moment the next phase closed and have stayed there since. Phase 11 and 12 reports were written without `REQUIREMENTS.md` in `covered_files` and did not inherit the trap.
- **Phase 11:** its `covered_files` legitimately list `Taskfile.yml` (home of `check:gonum`); Phase 12 added `docs:cli` / `docs:cli:drift` to the same file, so the covered digest no longer matches. That is the check working as designed over an unrelated edit.

No claim in any of the five reports is contradicted by later work — every phase's gates were re-run green during Phase 12's regression pass (`test:unit` 52/52, goldens, web 584/584, `proto:drift`, `web:drift`, `docs:cli:drift`, `check:gonum`, `check:no-force-layout`, graphcluster ancestry/digest tests). The tool-sanctioned repair is `/gsd-verify-work <phase>` re-verification per phase; this audit does not hand-edit the reports. `complete-milestone` must be run with this known.
