**MAINT-03's framing in 04-CONTEXT.md is narrower than the facts.** D-06 describes a
mismatch between `ci.yml` and `release.yml`. `ci.yml` never names a GoReleaser version at
all — its `goreleaser-check` job runs `task check:goreleaser`, which resolves transitively
through `go.tool.mod`. The two real pin sites are `go.tool.mod` and `release.yml`, and they
are not the same code path: `release.yml` installs a standalone prebuilt binary via
`goreleaser/goreleaser-action` for the actual release build, while `task check:goreleaser`
builds from the pinned modfile. The plan aligns the two *sites*, and the assertion in
Task 2 binds those two sites, not the phantom `ci.yml` literal.
